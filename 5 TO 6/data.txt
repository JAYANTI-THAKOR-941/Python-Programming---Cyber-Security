
Top Threats
Malware & Ransomware: Software that locks or harms files to demand payment.
Phishing: Fake messages meant to trick people into giving away passwords or money..
AI-Powered Attacks: Automated tools that make cyberattacks faster and harder to spot.

Common Types of Cybersecurity
Network Security: Guards internal networks from intruders and malware.
Cloud Security: Secures data and services kept on cloud platforms.
Protects individual devices like phones, laptops, and tablets.
Application Security: Fixes code flaws and protects software from threats..
